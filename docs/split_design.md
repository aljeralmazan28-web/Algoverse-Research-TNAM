
Held-out designs
1. Unseen cell lines: 70/15/15 group partition of cell lines into train/validation/test. Tissue × sex balance optimized subject to grouping, not assured. Main result.

2. Unseen drugs: hold out entire compounds; requires molecular representation to be a credible cold-drug evaluation. Sex/tissue balance arises via assigned drug measurements, not group-stratified sex labels.

3. Unseen tissues: hold out entire tissues. Tissue stratification is mathematically impossible here; ensure sex coverage per partition and state which tissues are withheld.

Primary comparisons
- H2: Baseline female-male RMSE gap and CI; interpretation is predictive disparity, not disease mechanism.
- H3: Proteomics vs no-proteomics on identical proteomics-overlap cells.
- H4: Same-feature early fusion without sex additions vs sex-aware add-ons, plus within-tissue sex-permutation negative control.
- H1, H5: teammate-generated annotation reconciliation and drug-ranking evidence only.
